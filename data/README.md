# Data source and attribution

`raw/hotel_bookings.csv` is the unchanged source CSV supplied with the capstone (119,390 rows and 32 columns).

- Dataset: [Hotel Booking Demand on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jessemostipak/hotel-booking-demand).
- Original research: Antonio, N., de Almeida, A., & Nunes, L. (2019). *Hotel booking demand datasets*. Data in Brief, 22, 41–49. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2018.11.126).
- The Kaggle dataset lists [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) as its license. Credit remains with the original authors and dataset contributors.
- SHA-256: `7c2ae42a7353905ea136e5c2287f17c92c5435826598bfbb8491c6f0c7b1fc06`.

The data cover arrivals at two Portuguese hotels from July 2015 through August 2017. The original article describes removal of hotel and customer identifiers. This is a historical research dataset, not current hotel operating data.

Prepared data are generated by notebook 01 under `outputs/data_preparation/`; scoring outputs are generated by notebook 02 under `outputs/modeling/`. Those files are transformations of the attributed source. Large reproducible outputs are excluded from Git to avoid keeping several copies of the same observations.

The dataset license does not automatically relicense the team's code, reports, or presentation; no new blanket license is asserted for those materials.
